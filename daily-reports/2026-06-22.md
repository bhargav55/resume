**Gap:** ZK is the single highest-paid skill in Web3 (2x salary premium). You have none on your resume.
**What to learn:**
- [Noir](https://noir-lang.org/) — easiest entry point, Aztec's ZK DSL, Rust-like syntax. Start here.

**Time to add to resume:** 4–6 weeks of focused weekend work
