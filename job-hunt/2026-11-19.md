- The fastest-growing areas are **smart contract security**, protocol design, L2/cross-chain and ZK.

## Daily improvement plan

**Resume and positioning (do this first)**
1. Lead with a one-line pitch: *"Protocol engineer who has shipped on-chain perps (CLOB, liquidations, oracle integrations) through Quantstamp and Zellic audits, and builds AI-agent infrastructure."* AI plus onchain is now a strong selling point.
2. Add numbers: TVL, volume, users, gas saved, and how many audit findings you fixed. Right now only the indexer bullet has a number.
3. Link GitHub repos and audit reports, and put `oddz.fi`, the Nunchi docs and OrgGPT near the top.

**AI-first workflow (most companies now ask about this)**
- Use Claude Code or Cursor every day for Foundry test generation, invariant and fuzz scaffolding, and Slither triage. Write a short blog post or tweet thread showing the workflow, since recruiters look for this proof.
- Build or contribute to an **MCP server for an onchain protocol** (for example, read positions and simulate trades with Foundry/anvil). It's a small portfolio project that combines your three strengths.
- Try LLM-assisted auditing tools and write about how they compare with Slither on a known-buggy repo.

**Upskilling (pick one per week)**
- **Security:** compete in Code4rena, Sherlock or Cantina contests. Even one valid High finding is strong for your resume, and it's the fastest-growing hiring area.
- **Formal verification:** Halmos, Certora or Kontrol invariants for vault and perps math.
- **Modern EVM:** Uniswap v4 hooks, EIP-7702 (it builds on your 4337 work), transient storage (EIP-1153), and intents and cross-chain flows (ERC-7683, LayerZero/CCIP).
- **ZK basics:** Noir or SP1 coprocessors are increasingly mentioned in protocol roles.
- **Rust:** keep the SVM skills current. One small Anchor or Pinocchio program per month keeps you eligible for Solana perps roles.

**Daily routine (~1 hr)**
- 15 min: apply to the 2–3 best leads above with a tailored first line
- 30 min: one audit-contest issue, or one Foundry invariant test
- 15 min: post a short technical note on X or LinkedIn (people who post get more inbound recruiter messages)
