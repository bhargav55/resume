## Is your skill set outdated?

**No.** Perps, ERC-4337, ERC-4626, RWA/ERC-3643, ERC-8004/8183 agents and RAG are all current in 2026. Security is the most in-demand skill area, with several openings for every qualified candidate. These gaps would make you stand out:

- **Security depth:** invariant/fuzz testing (Foundry invariants, Echidna, Medusa) and formal verification (Certora, Halmos). Join Code4rena, Sherlock or Cantina contests; leaderboard placements are the strongest signal for security-heavy roles.
- **ZK verification onchain:** Groth16/PLONK verifier contracts, SP1 and RISC Zero. Perp DEXs such as Hibachi now ask for this.
- **EIP-7702** (delegated EOAs) alongside your ERC-4337 work, and **Uniswap v4 hooks**.
- **Cross-chain:** LayerZero, Chainlink CCIP and Hyperlane messaging.
- **HyperEVM / Hyperliquid** integrations, given your perps background.
