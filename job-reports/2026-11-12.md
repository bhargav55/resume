## Today's upskilling focus
1. **Make the AI-first angle visible.** About 53% of web3 postings now mention AI, and Solidity+AI appears in about 20% (CryptoJobsList 2026 report). Add a resume line on AI in your dev workflow: Claude Code/Cursor for writing Foundry invariant tests, LLM-assisted triage of audit findings, MCP tools on top of your indexer.
2. **Agent payments and wallets.** ERC-4337 + EIP-7702 smart accounts for agents, x402 or other agent payment rails, session keys. You already have ERC-8004/8183, so this rounds it out. Build a small demo of an agent with a paymaster-sponsored smart account.
3. **Security depth.** Security roles have more openings than qualified candidates. Do a public Code4rena/Cantina/Sherlock contest, add Halmos/Certora or Kontrol formal verification, and use Echidna/Medusa for fuzzing.
4. **Resume fixes.** Add metrics: TVL, volume, gas saved. Put EIP-7702 and Uniswap v4 hooks in the skills section if you've used them. The "Nunchi: Jul '25 – May '26" end date shows a gap, so a short 'currently building OrgGPT' line helps.
