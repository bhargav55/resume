## Where to improve — daily upskilling plan

**1. Your strongest current differentiator: onchain AI agents.** ERC-8004
(agent identity/reputation) only reached mainnet in January 2026, and you
already shipped an ERC-8004/8183 agent marketplace in production at
Nunchi.trade — very few Solidity engineers can say that yet. Don't let this
sit buried in a bullet point:
- Write one public technical post or short repo walkthrough on the agent
  marketplace design (registration, proof-of-completion, incentive claims).
  This is the single highest-leverage thing you can do this week — it's a
  rare, timely, in-demand combination (Solidity + agent economics).
- Actively search "ERC-8004", "agent marketplace", "x402", "agentic commerce"
  on web3.career/cryptojobslist daily — this niche is moving faster than
  general job-board categories update.

**2. AI-first hiring is already here — bring AI into your own workflow, not just your resume.**
- Use an LLM-assisted review pass (Claude/GPT) as a *pre-audit* step before
  Slither/Trail of Bits on any contract you touch — write up before/after
  findings, it's a concrete talking point in interviews ("AI-augmented audit
  workflow").
- Try LLM-driven Foundry fuzz-harness / invariant-test generation from NatSpec
  — teams are explicitly screening for engineers who "write tests that prove
  security assumptions hold under adversarial conditions" (recurring phrase
  in current JDs).
- Your OrgGPT RAG/multi-agent experience is a real asset for "AI-first"
  companies — don't undersell it as a side project; frame it as evidence you
  can ship agentic systems end-to-end, not just consume an LLM API.

**3. Public reputation signals hiring managers actually check.** Search
results confirm teams look at Code4rena/Sherlock leaderboards, GitHub, and
Etherscan-deployed contracts before a first call.
- Enter one Code4rena or Sherlock contest this month if you haven't recently
  — even a mid-tier placement is a concrete, checkable credential.
- Make sure GitHub has recent, readable commits (not just private-repo work
  from employers) — pin the agent-marketplace or vault work if you can
  open-source a sanitized version.

**4. Nothing here looks outdated** — ERC-4337, ERC-4626, ERC-3643, upgradeable
proxies, Foundry/Hardhat, Slither are all still exactly what current JDs ask
for. The gap isn't skills, it's visibility: keep LinkedIn/GitHub headline
updated to lead with "Solidity + onchain agents (ERC-8004)" since that's the
freshest, least-common pairing in your background right now.
