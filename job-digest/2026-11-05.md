## Resume tweaks
- Lead the summary with quantified results: TVL/volume secured, audit findings fixed (Quantstamp, Zellic), events/min, gas savings.
- Add Foundry invariant/fuzz testing and fork testing explicitly. Several JDs ask for them.
- Move the AI/agents section (OrgGPT, ERC-8004/8183) near the top for AI-first companies.
- Add public links: audit reports, GitHub repos, any CTF or Code4rena/Sherlock results.

## Skill recommendations (today's focus)
1. **Invariant testing and symbolic tools**: Foundry invariant suites, Echidna/Medusa, Halmos. Baseline expectation for audits and protocol teams in 2026.
2. **Formal verification basics**: Certora CVL on one of your own contracts (the vault or staking accumulator).
3. **AI-assisted dev workflow**: use Claude Code with a Foundry project for test scaffolding, pre-audit review and Slither triage. Verify every finding with deterministic tools (PoC test). Put the workflow in your resume and README.
4. **Competitive audits**: 1 Code4rena/Sherlock/Cantina contest per month builds public credibility.
5. **Current protocol designs**: Aave V4 (hub/spoke), Uniswap v4 hooks, Morpho Blue, intents and account abstraction (EIP-7702).
6. **Rust/SVM**: keep Anchor current as a secondary skill. EVM is the market for your profile.
