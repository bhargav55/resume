## Resume tips
- Lead with quantified outcomes: TVL, volume, audit findings closed, latency. Most bullets have no numbers.
- Add viem/ethers/wagmi to Skills. Several TS roles ask for them by name.
- Name audit firms and severity counts. Zellic and Quantstamp are strong signals.

## Upskill (today)
- Invariant and fuzz testing: Foundry invariants, Echidna, Medusa. Security-focused roles expect these.
- Formal verification: Certora or Halmos.
- Read one recent audit report (Code4rena or Sherlock) and note the bug class.
- AI in the workflow: use Claude Code or an agent with Foundry to generate invariant tests and review diffs. Your OrgGPT and ERC-8004 agent work already fits the AI-first theme, so promote it.
- Newer EVM topics: EIP-7702 account abstraction, transient storage (EIP-1153), L2 and rollup tooling.
